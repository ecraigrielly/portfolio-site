About me

I'm a bit of a weirdo. The good kind.

I'm a Principal UX Designer with 25+ years of making complicated things feel simple. Good design makes life a little easier.

That's the whole thing for me.

Range
In-house, agency, freelance: I've done all three. I read rooms fast, work within real constraints, and ship things that stick.
Sweet spot
Design systems and accessibility, built in from day one. I built Borealis at Alaska Airlines from zero, merged five design systems into one at Gap, and ran live accessibility testing with blind users. I've also mentored designers finding their path.
Lately
AI, mostly. I'm making a design system AI-ready as a volunteer, built a job-search agent called Jobbie, and prototype in Claude Code and Figma Make.
Off the clock
Former roller derby skater. Current rink enthusiast. I skated roller derby for a local team and still hit the rink when my knees allow. Skating and design reward the same thing: falling down and getting right back up.
